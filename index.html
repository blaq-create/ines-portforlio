EDUCATION
Sample 01

Clear English Explanation: What Is a Clause?

A clause is a group of words that contains a subject and a verb, and it forms the basic building block of a sentence.

Independent Clause: Can stand alone as a complete sentence. Example: "I am tired."

Dependent Clause: Cannot stand alone. Example: "because I did not sleep well."

Understanding clauses helps you write clearly, speak confidently, and structure your ideas professionally.

Professional Communication: Announcement of a Small Change in Schedule

A professional communication sample demonstrating how to clearly communicate a schedule change while maintaining a polite and professional tone.

Subject: Update on Meeting Schedule

Dear Team,

I hope this message finds you well. I would like to inform you that our weekly team meeting originally scheduled for Thursday at 10:00 AM has been moved to Friday at 11:00 AM.
